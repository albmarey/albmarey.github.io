Dusty
Dusty is a non-commercial third-person puzzle game in which you use an extendable staff to manipulate light. The player explores ruins and unravels the mysteries of the past through the notes in their notebook.
I took on various roles during development as part of a 30-person team, primarily working as a Game Designer, Cinematics Designer, QA and Marketing.

Features

  • Third person
  • Jump with staff
  • Puzzle system based on light
  • Linear levels
  • Rich narrative
  • AI fauna
