Bottleneck PC
Bottleneck PC is a prototype designed for websites that sell hardware components, such as PC Componentes. The aim was to gamify the experience to make it easier to buy parts. We decided to use Unreal Engine 5.2 given our knowledge of the engine and because our next project would be in Unreal Engine 5.
During this project, I took on the role of Technical Designer.
My Contributions
  • I helped to design part of the concept for the experience.
  • Implementation of core systems with Blueprints:
    • Created a system for moving cameras between elements on the stage. I’m currently applying what I learnt to the Unreal Engine RPG project.
    • Adjusting the lighting on this level.
    • Drag and drop hardware components onto the stage.
    • Instance creation of hardware components following their purchase.
  • Created the outline and the blueprints to present it.
  • I set up the scene for the prototype by adapting the level design to the camera angles.
