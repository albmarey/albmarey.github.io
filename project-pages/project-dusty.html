Dusty
Dusty is a non-commercial third-person puzzle game in which you use an extendable staff to manipulate light developed with Unreal Engine 5.3. The player explores ruins and unravels the mysteries of the past through the notes in their notebook.
This was my final Master’s project at U-tad, where we set up a studio for nine months as part of a team of 30 people.
I took on various roles during development as part of a 30-person team, primarily working as a Game Designer, Technical Designer and Narrative.

Features

  • Third person
  • Jump with staff
  • Puzzle system based on light
  • Linear levels
  • Rich narrative
  • AI fauna
My contributions
  • Designed and prototyped core mechanchics and systems:
    • Designed a puzzle system based on magnetic cubes and light, using an extendable rod.
    • Based on the stick controls, I implemented the conditions for performing jumps with physics at specific points on the game map.
  • Designed and written the narrative:
    • I wrote the full story of the game from start to finish to guide the narrative and art teams.
    • Worked with the art team to decide on character and environment designs based on the requirements of the plot, the world and the animations.
    • Structured the ways in which the player could discover the story and lore during a playthrough.
  • Designed and Implemented the User Interface (UI) and UX:
    • Implemented main menu (New Game, Continue, Credits, Quit), Notebook Menu, responsiveness.
    • Implemented the tutorial and a Tooltip System to show controls during the playthrough.
    • Designed the control layout for the control panel and keyboard.
  • Designed and Implemented the Cinematics Tráiler y cuatro más.
    • Created all the cutscenes in the game using Level Sequencer.
    • Developed and implemented a trigger system to play cutscenes using Blueprints and C++.
    • I created the trailer for the game’s release and published it alongside the Steam page
  • Marketing
    • Successfully pitched the game to different stakeholders.
    • Ran the social media marketing campaign alongside the executive producer.
    • At video game trade fairs, I was responsible for setting up the stands and carrying out testing plans involving up to 100 players.
